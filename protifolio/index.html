Professional Profile

I am a Senior Video Editor and Visual Creator with four years of hands-on experience in video editing and post-production, supported by five years of experience in photography, studio lighting, and visual production. I approach every project as a complete visual process: understanding the idea, shaping the story, planning the look, creating or capturing the visuals, and refining the final result through editing, color grading, motion design, and visual effects.

My work combines creative thinking with technical execution. I use professional editing tools alongside modern AI platforms to develop content from scratch, enhance production workflows, explore visual concepts, and turn creative briefs into polished images and videos.

Core Areas of Expertise

  • Video Editing & Storytelling: Cutting, sequencing, pacing, transitions, narrative flow, and polished final delivery.
  • Color Correction & Grading: Exposure, contrast, color balance, cinematic looks, mood creation, and shot matching.
  • Motion Graphics & Animation: Animated typography, graphic elements, visual explanations, and motion-based storytelling.
  • 3D & Visual Effects: Blender-based 3D scenes, compositing, VFX integration, and visual enhancement.
  • AI Content Production: Generating images and videos from scratch, visual development, and AI-assisted creative workflows.
  • Photography & Lighting: Studio photography, lighting setups, visual composition, and commercial image-making.
